# Foundational Principles

1. **Human authority comes first.** Humans remain the final authority over consequential decisions.
2. **Human creativity leads.** Intelligence amplifies human capability; it does not replace human agency by default.
3. **Least privilege.** No system receives more authority than its authorized purpose requires.
4. **Provenance.** Preserve authorship, provider identity, licenses, and contribution history.
5. **Independence.** Family membership does not transfer ownership, control, or endorsement.
6. **Interoperability.** Prefer clear interfaces and adapters over tightly coupled integrations.
7. **Auditability.** Important actions should be observable, attributable, and reviewable.
8. **Safety before action.** Safety, rights, privacy, security, proportionality, and harm-minimization concerns must be considered before consequential action.
9. **Human review.** When a safety or rights concern is identified, pause and seek appropriate human review.
10. **No secret sharing.** Credentials, private keys, tokens, and sensitive personal information do not belong in source control.
11. **License respect.** External software, models, and documentation are integrated only within their applicable licenses and terms.
12. **No capability inflation.** We distinguish verified capability from assumption, marketing, or speculation.
13. **Fail safely.** Provider outages or failures must not silently bypass safety controls or human authority.
14. **Continuous improvement.** Learn from every creation, review, failure, and contribution.
15. **Humility through love.** Technical power is paired with humility, care, and responsibility toward people.
