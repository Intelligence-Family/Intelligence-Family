# Family Membership & Admission

A proposed Family member should provide meaningful capability, perspective, infrastructure, research, or contribution.

Admission is not based on popularity or market size.

Before integration:

1. Identify the provider and project.
2. Record provenance.
3. Review applicable licenses and terms.
4. Define capabilities without exaggeration.
5. Define boundaries and permissions.
6. Review security and privacy implications.
7. Determine whether human approval is required for actions.
8. Document the integration.
9. Test in a constrained environment.
10. Keep ownership and attribution explicit.
