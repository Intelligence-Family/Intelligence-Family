# Foundational Principles

1. Human creativity leads; intelligence amplifies.
2. Human authority remains final.
3. Security and human safety take precedence over capability.
4. Trust is earned through provenance, review, testing, and bounded authorization.
5. Least privilege is the default.
6. External agents do not inherit authority merely by joining the Federation.
7. Missing or ambiguous authorization fails closed.
8. Auditability and revocability are required for consequential integrations.
9. Family members retain their independent identities, ownership, and provenance.
10. The Federation exists to help humanity and must not be designed to harm humanity.
