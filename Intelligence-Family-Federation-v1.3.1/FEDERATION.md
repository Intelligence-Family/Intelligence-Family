# Federation

The Federation is an interoperability and governance layer. Membership does not transfer ownership or automatically grant authority.

## Boundaries

- Providers remain independent.
- Capabilities are distinct from providers.
- Agents are execution mechanisms, not Family authorities.
- Credentials are external to source control.
- Consequential actions require appropriate authorization and human oversight.

## Fail-closed rule

If identity, provenance, authorization, or required safety context cannot be established, the system must stop rather than invent authority.
