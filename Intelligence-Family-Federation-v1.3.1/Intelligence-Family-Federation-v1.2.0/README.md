# Intelligence Family 🌎❤️🦾

**Different minds. Shared purpose.**  
**Human creativity leads. Intelligence amplifies.**  
**Humility through love.**  
**For the Betterment of Humanity.**

The Intelligence Family is a federation layer for independent Intelligence systems, providers, capabilities, skills, and agent tooling. It enables collaboration without surrendering human control.

## Governance boundary
Family membership does not grant authority, ownership, endorsement, employment, or affiliation. Human authority remains final. External systems and model outputs are treated as untrusted until validated at the appropriate boundary.

## Repository map
- `Intelligence-Family-Federation/` — mission, principles, family tree, architecture, federation, security, provenance, and governance.
- `.github/` — repository automation and contribution controls.
- `docs/` — operational and governance documentation.
